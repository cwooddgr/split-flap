Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Flip Flap ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use Flip Flap on Apple TV, the web display at flipflap.dgrlabs.co, or the web remote that opens when you scan a display's QR code.

Information We Collect

The App collects minimal information necessary for functionality:

  • Anonymous Authentication: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to sign each display and remote in without an account. Firebase creates a random identifier for that anonymous session. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number, and we have no way to connect the identifier to you.
  • Message Content: Messages you send to a display are stored for a short time in Firebase Firestore. We store the text, the time it was sent, and which button sent it, under a random room ID. We don't store any identifier for you or your device with the message.
  • Room IDs: A display makes up a random room ID each time you launch the app or load the display page. The ID is not linked to your identity.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Contact information

Data Storage and Retention

  • Messages are stored on Firebase cloud servers
  • A room and its message are deleted 7 days after the last message sent to that room. The deletion is automatic and can take up to a day to happen after the 7 days are up
  • We keep no other record of messages
  • Data is processed in accordance with Google Firebase's security practices

Third-Party Services

The App uses Firebase (Google) for:

  • Anonymous authentication
  • Real-time message synchronization

Like any online service, Firebase sees basic connection details such as your IP address when your device talks to it, as described in Google's policy.

Firebase's privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. A message travels from the remote to our Firebase database, and from there to the display showing that room.

A room ID works like a key. Anyone who has it, for example anyone who scanned the display's QR code, can read and change that room's message until the display starts a new room. Please don't put anything private on the board.

Links on the Remote Page

The web remote shows a link to another DGR Labs app. The App Store badge image loads from Apple. If you tap the link, it goes through our own short-link service, which records the click along with your country and city as estimated by our hosting provider, your browser's user-agent string, and the site you came from. It does not record your IP address or your room ID.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App contains no user accounts, social features, or mechanisms for children to share personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
