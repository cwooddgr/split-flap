### Description (4000 characters max)
Flip Flap turns your Apple TV into a stunning retro split-flap message board — the kind you'd find in train stations and airports. Type a message from your phone and watch each tile click and flip into place with satisfying mechanical sounds.

Getting started is simple: launch Flip Flap on your Apple TV and scan the QR code with your phone. No app to install on your phone — just scan and type. Your message appears on the big screen in seconds, animated tile by tile with authentic clacking sounds.

FEATURES

- Realistic split-flap animation with mechanical sound effects
- 21×8 tile grid for large, readable messages
- Phone remote via QR code — no extra app needed
- Word-wrap and auto-centering for clean layouts
- Random quote and funny quote buttons for instant content
- Press the center button on your Apple TV remote to hide the QR code for a clean display
- Prevents screen sleep so your message stays visible
- Works on Apple TV HD and all Apple TV 4K models

PERFECT FOR

- Welcome messages for guests and visitors
- Motivational quotes in your office or living room
- Event signage and party displays
- Restaurant or cafe specials
- Fun family message board

Flip Flap connects securely through the cloud so your phone remote works from anywhere on any network. Messages are encrypted in transit and rooms expire automatically.

### Notes for Reviewers
Flip Flap requires no account or sign-in. On launch, a QR code appears in the bottom-right corner of the screen. Scan it with a phone camera to open the web-based remote control. Type a message and tap "Display" to see it animate on the Apple TV. You can also tap "Random Quote" or "Funny Quote" for preset content. Press the center button on the Apple TV remote to hide/show the QR code. The app requires an internet connection to sync messages via Firebase.
