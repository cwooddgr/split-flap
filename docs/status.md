## tvOS App

Current version: **1.2** (build 3; tag `tvos-1.2-3`), **live on the App Store since 2026-09-21** (submitted 8:36 AM MDT that day and approved the same afternoon; `READY_FOR_SALE` read from the App Store Connect API at 4:07 PM MDT, and the public App Store lookup shows 1.2 with a release date of 2:26 PM MDT). The 1.2 version record has the build attached, 1.1's promotional text copied over unchanged (a new version does not inherit it), a What's New Charlie approved as written, and the corrected Apple TV Privacy Policy text (all decided-by-user 2026-09-21, along with the submission itself and his word that the day on his Apple TV went fine). Build numbers are per version on tvOS, so 1.1 (3) and 1.2 (3) are different binaries. The submission itself was three App Store Connect calls (POST `/v1/reviewSubmissions`, POST `/v1/reviewSubmissionItems`, PATCH `submitted: true`); on 2026-09-21 the auto mode classifier refused them from the agent, and Charlie got them through himself. Later that day he stated a standing permission for these calls (see "tvOS release process" below). What 1.2 holds beyond 1.1: the audio session is set to `.ambient` at launch so the app no longer stops the user's music (1.1 did, at the first flap; confirmed on a device), the audio engine restarts after a route change (never reproduced), and the layout is the new fixture-tested port (breaks long words across lines, folds accents, no triple spaces). Also in 1.2, on `main` since 2026-09-20 (scope decided-by-user that day, his items "1-4 plus 5 and 8"): the connection scaffolding in `RoomViewModel.swift` is gone (sign-in and the listener retry with backoff, one listener, never a sign-out), Firestore uses a memory-only cache, the Canvas draws from a main-thread copy of the board state, the QR image is built once, a "Reconnecting…" line shows at the bottom left after 5 seconds of trouble, and everything on screen drifts around a 6-point circle, one step every 3 minutes, against burn-in. **The QR code stays up until the user hides it; don't add an auto-hide.** Built and run in the simulator only, apart from the music fix, which Charlie confirmed on his Apple TV. Bundle ID `co.dgrlabs.flipflap`, deployment target tvOS 17.0.
